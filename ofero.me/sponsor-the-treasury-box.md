# Sponsor the Treasury Box

## **Sponsorship Structure**

1. **Sponsorship Package**
   * **Cost:** Fixed at $1,000 USD, payable in USDC or OFE tokens based on the current exchange rate.
   * **Duration:** Sponsorships start on the 1st of each month and end on the last day of the month.
   * **Capacity**: Up to 10 sponsors per month. If the current month is full, your sponsorship will be scheduled for the next available month.
2. **What You Get**
   * Your brand’s logo (provided in `.png` format, landscape-oriented, maximum width of 294px) displayed in the Treasury Box interface with a link to your website.
   * Randomized appearance during the month across users opening boxes.
   * There is no fixed duration or number of views guaranteed due to the decentralized and variable nature of the Treasury Box.

***

**Steps to Become a Sponsor**

1. **Prepare Your Submission**
   * Email your request to info@ofero.network.
   * Include:
     * Your logo in `.png` format, landscape-oriented with a maximum width of 294px.
     * The link to your website.
     * The wallet address you will use for payment.
     * The number of sponsorship packages you wish to purchase (e.g., 1 package = $1,000 USD).
2. **Review and Approval**
   * Submissions will be reviewed to ensure compliance with Ofero Network guidelines.
   * We **do not accept sponsorships from the following categories**:
     * Pornographic content.
     * Illegal activities.
3. **Payment Instructions**
   * Approved sponsors will receive instructions to send tokens to the Treasury Box wallet: `erd1y2vj7jqypaxzz3xwpylnvrs3tzttgjcpnyu4pur7lzz5pqkmhtys0treas`
4. **Logo Activation**
   * Upon confirmation of payment, your logo will be published and included in the Treasury Box rotation.

***

### **Benefits of Sponsoring**

* Increased visibility among a blockchain-savvy audience.
* Association with a trusted and innovative ecosystem.
* Decentralized exposure to users worldwide.

***

### **Notes**

* Sponsorships are handled on a **first-come, first-served** basis.
* Sponsorship does not influence the outcome or content of Treasury Box rewards.
* Ofero Network reserves the right to remove any sponsor in violation of its guidelines without refund.
