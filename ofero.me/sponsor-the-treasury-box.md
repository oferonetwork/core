# Sponsor the Treasury Box

## **Sponsorship Structure**

1. **Sponsorship Package**
   * **Cost:** Fixed at $1,000 USD, payable in USDC or OFE tokens based on the current exchange rate.
   * **Duration:** Sponsorship includes 10,000 guaranteed views. Each view costs $0.10. Once the 10,000 views are reached, the sponsorship ends.
   * **Capacity**: Up to 10 sponsors at a time. If the current slot is full, your sponsorship will be scheduled for the next available period.
2. **What You Get**
   * Your brand’s logo (provided in `.png` format, landscape-oriented, maximum width of 294px) displayed in the Treasury Box interface with a link to your website.
   * Displayed in a rotational sequence, ensuring equal exposure across users opening boxes.
   * Guaranteed 10,000 views per sponsorship package.

***

**How to Become a Sponsor**

1. **Prepare Your Submission**
   * Email your request to info@ofero.network.
   * Include:
     * Your logo in `.png` format, landscape-oriented with a maximum width of 294px.
     * The link to your website and a short description that you would like to add under your logo.
     * The wallet address you will use for payment.
     * The number of sponsorship packages you wish to purchase (e.g., 1 package = $1,000 USD).
2. **Review and Approval**
   * Submissions will be reviewed to ensure compliance with Ofero Network guidelines.
   * We **do not accept sponsorships from the following categories**:
     * Pornographic content.
     * Illegal activities.
3. **Payment Instructions**
   * Approved sponsors will receive instructions to send tokens DIRECTLY to the Treasury Box wallet: `erd1y2vj7jqypaxzz3xwpylnvrs3tzttgjcpnyu4pur7lzz5pqkmhtys0treas`
4. **Logo Activation**
   * Upon confirmation of payment, your logo will be published and included in the Treasury Box rotation for the next month.

{% hint style="info" %}
Sponsorships are handled on a **first-come, first-served** basis.
{% endhint %}
