# Overview

## Key Features

1. **User Identification**:
   * Utilizes NFC chips and QR codes to facilitate seamless user identification.
   * Allows businesses to verify users through their Ofero ID Cards, ensuring secure and straightforward interactions.
2. **Reward System**:
   * Offers blockchain-based rewards to users based on loyalty and engagement.
   * Rewards include OFE tokens that can be used within the Ofero ecosystem.
3. **Business Integration**:
   * Automatically adds businesses based on user interest and engagement.

## Benefits

* **For Users**:
  * Easy-to-use system for earning and redeeming rewards.
  * Access to exclusive discounts and offers from partner businesses.
  * Opportunity to influence which businesses join the platform.
* **For Businesses**:
  * Increased visibility and customer engagement.
  * Decentralized voting improves transparency in rankings.
  * Cost-effective way to connect with a loyal and active user base.
