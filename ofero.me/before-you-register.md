# Before You Register

Before you proceed with registration, here are some essential steps to ensure a smooth and rewarding experience:

1. **Connect Locally**\
   Ofero Network thrives on community connections. If you don't already know someone from your local area who is part of Ofero Network, we recommend joining our official Telegram channel at [@oferonetwork](https://t.me/oferonetwork).
2. **Find a Local Member**\
   On Telegram, you can ask for someone in your area who is part of the Ofero Network. This connection is valuable because registering under their referral link enhances the trust and collaboration that defines our ecosystem.
3. **Register Through a Referral**\
   Once you find a local member, they can provide their referral link for your registration. This process ensures you become part of a connected network that supports each other's growth.

### **Why Registering Through a Referral Matters:**

* **Stronger Community Ties**: Starting under a trusted member from your area helps build local relationships and enhances collaboration.
* **Optimized Benefits**: Gain immediate access to network guidance and opportunities that may not be available to independent registrations.
* **Support System**: Your local referrer can guide you through the platform and answer questions to get you started on the right path.

If you have any questions or need help connecting with someone, feel free to reach out to our team on [Telegram ](https://t.me/oferonetwork)or email us at [info@ofero.network](mailto:info@ofero.network).
