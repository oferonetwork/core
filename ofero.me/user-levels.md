# User Levels

### 1. Free Membership

All users start with a **Free Membership** upon joining the Ofero Network. This level provides basic access to the platform, allowing users to explore and engage with its features at no cost.

#### Features:

* Access to the Ofero.me platform.
* Basic participation in community activities.
* Limited access to rewards and discounts.

***

### 2. PLUS Membership

For users seeking more engagement and benefits, the **PLUS Membership** offers enhanced privileges. This level requires a subscription fee of:

* **$5 per month**, or
* **$50 per year**.

#### Benefits:

* Access to exclusive discounts and promotions.
* Increased rewards from the Treasury Box.
* Priority support within the ecosystem.

***

### 3. VIP Membership

The **VIP Membership** is designed for those who own a **Semi-Fungible Token (SFT)** within the Ofero Network. VIP members enjoy the highest level of access and privileges.

#### Benefits:

* All benefits of PLUS Membership.
* Governance participation, including voting rights on critical network decisions.
* Special recognition within the community.
* Unique opportunities, such as early access to events or exclusive partnerships.

***

#### How to Upgrade:

* **PLUS Membership**: Sign up for the subscription plan on Ofero.me.
* **VIP Membership**: Purchase an SFT through the official channels when available.

***

By offering these distinct levels, the Ofero Network ensures that every user can choose the membership that best aligns with their goals and engagement preferences. Whether you're exploring blockchain technology or actively participating in shaping the ecosystem, there's a place for you in the Ofero community.
