# Quickstart Guide

#### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [**Ofero Network's main platform (ofero.me)**](https://ofero.me).
2. Click on the **Login / Register** button and provide the necessary details:
   * **Name**
   * **Email Address**
   * **Password**
3. Verify your email address through the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
4. Log in to access your account dashboard.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/login.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

1. After logging in, navigate to **Profile Settings**.
2. Add important details such as:
   * A display name for others to recognize you.
   * An optional profile picture to personalize your presence.
3. Save your changes.

#### Step 3: Explore the Ecosystem

1. **Connections**:
   * Connect to a leader or build your network using the **Connections System**.
   * Strengthen your ties within the Ofero community.
2. **Vault**:
   * Secure your tokens and explore features like staking or other financial tools.
   * (Optional: Deposit tokens to access premium functionalities.)
3. **Special Offers**:
   * Discover and redeem instant discounts or loyalty rewards from our partners.
4. **Add Businesses** (Optional):
   * If you're a business owner, list your shop or service and start engaging with the community.

#### Step 4: Learn and Earn

1. Read through the **FAQs** or **Documentation** to deepen your understanding of Ofero Network.
2. Engage in community discussions and share ideas with other members.
3. Keep an eye on upcoming promotions, events, and updates.

#### Step 5: Stay Connected

* Follow Ofero Network on Social Media for updates, tips, and announcements.
* Join our Community Forum or Telegram Group to engage with other members and get support.

### Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or have questions, visit our **Support Center** or contact us directly at **support@ofero.me**.

Congratulations! You're now ready to explore the full potential of Ofero Network. Dive into the features, make connections, and enjoy the journey!
