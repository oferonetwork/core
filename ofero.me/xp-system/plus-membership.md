# PLUS Membership

While your PLUS membership is active, you receive:

**×2 XP**

on almost every XP action.

### What Does PLUS Double?

The ×2 multiplier applies to XP generated from activities such as:

* Daily activity
* Partner check-ins
* Games
* Surveys
* Events
* Cashback
* Feedback
* Shop discovery
* Sending OFE
* Eligible Network Bonus XP

### The Two Exceptions

Two rewards are not doubled:

**Buying or renewing PLUS → 400 XP**

**Your referral buying or renewing PLUS → 400 XP**

These rewards remain fixed at 400 XP.

### PLUS and Rings

Every PLUS member inside a Ring adds:

**+5%**

to the Ring bonus.

This benefits everyone in that Ring.

### PLUS and Your Network

PLUS also increases eligible Network Bonus XP received from your referrals.

With PLUS, your received Network Bonus uses:

**×1.2 on Level 1**

and:

**×1.1 on Level 2**

### PLUS and Builder

PLUS is also required for the Builder badge.

To become a Builder, a chain of three active PLUS members must exist:

**You → Your Referral → Their Referral**

When all three have PLUS, the member at the top of the chain can qualify for Builder.

### Referral PLUS Rewards

If someone you directly referred buys or renews PLUS, you can receive:

**400 XP**

for each purchase or renewal.

However, **your own PLUS must be active at the moment they make the purchase**.

### Live Multiplier

The PLUS multiplier works while your membership is active.

If PLUS expires, your next XP action returns to its normal multiplier.

XP you already earned is never reduced.

**PLUS turns regular activity into ×2 XP while also improving other parts of your Ofero network.**
