# Flow

Every day you return to Ofero, you can build your Flow and increase the XP earned from selected activities.

The maximum Flow multiplier is:

**×1.50 at 30 days**

### Flow Levels

| Flow    | XP Multiplier |
| ------- | ------------- |
| 2 days  | ×1.05         |
| 3 days  | ×1.08         |
| 5 days  | ×1.12         |
| 7 days  | ×1.16         |
| 9 days  | ×1.20         |
| 12 days | ×1.25         |
| 15 days | ×1.30         |
| 19 days | ×1.35         |
| 23 days | ×1.40         |
| 26 days | ×1.45         |
| 30 days | **×1.50**     |

Your Flow can continue growing beyond 30 days, but the multiplier remains at ×1.50.

### Where Does Flow Apply?

The Flow multiplier applies to:

* Daily login
* Crack the Code
* Crack the Word
* Crack the Word win bonus
* Event RSVP

It does not apply to check-ins, PLUS purchases, PLUS referral rewards, cashback, surveys, feedback, shop discovery, or sending OFE.

### Missing a Day

Missing one day does **not** reset your Flow.

Instead, one day is removed from your Flow for each day you miss.

For example:

**40-day Flow → 3 missed days → 37-day Flow**

Your Flow never drops below 1.

This also means that building a Flow beyond 30 days can give you some protection. If your Flow is well above 30, you can miss a few days and still remain above the level required for the maximum ×1.50 multiplier.

### When Does a New Day Start?

Ofero uses **UTC calendar days**.

This means the daily reset does not necessarily happen at midnight in your country.

The same UTC day system is also used for the daily Shop Discovery reward.

### Keep Your Flow

You do not need hundreds of consecutive days to reach the maximum XP benefit.

The target is simple:

**Reach 30 days → unlock ×1.50 → keep coming back.**
