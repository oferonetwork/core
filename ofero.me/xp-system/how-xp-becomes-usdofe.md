# How XP Becomes $OFE

Instead of using a fixed conversion such as "1,000 XP = X OFE," Ofero uses a **daily reward pool**.

This means XP represents your share of the activity for that day.

### Daily Distribution

Every day has an OFE reward pool.

Your share is calculated using:

**Your Reward = Daily Pool × (Your XP ÷ Everyone's XP)**

Only XP earned during that specific day is used for that day's distribution.

### Simple Example

Imagine the daily reward pool is 10,000 OFE.

If all eligible members generate:

**1,000,000 XP**

and you generate:

**10,000 XP**

you generated 1% of that day's eligible XP.

Your share would therefore be:

**1% of the daily pool**

The example is only meant to explain how the calculation works. The actual result depends on the real daily pool and the XP generated by eligible members that day.

### Lifetime XP vs Daily XP

Your lifetime XP total does not determine your daily OFE reward.

The system looks at:

**Your XP today vs. everyone's XP today**

Then a new calculation starts for the next day.

### Why Consistency Matters

Each day has its own reward pool.

This means regular activity gives you the opportunity to participate in multiple daily distributions instead of concentrating all your activity into one day.

### Quiet and Busy Days

The value represented by one XP is not fixed.

When total network XP is lower, each XP represents a larger part of that day's pool.

When total network XP is higher, each XP represents a smaller part.

For this reason, Ofero cannot promise a fixed OFE value for a specific amount of XP.

### When Are Rewards Distributed?

After a UTC day ends, its XP is settled the following morning.

The day's XP is frozen at:

**00:05 UTC**

The corresponding OFE distribution is sent at:

**11:00 UTC**

### The Simple Rule

There is no permanent fixed exchange rate between XP and OFE.

Your goal is to increase your share through activity:

**Earn XP → Use your multipliers → Participate in the daily pool → Receive your share in OFE.**
