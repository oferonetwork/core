# XP System

You can earn XP by opening Ofero daily, checking in at partner businesses, playing games, answering questions, joining events, using OFE, receiving cashback, and more.

The more active you are, the more XP you can collect.

But activity is only one part of the system.

Your XP can also be increased through **Flow, Rings, the Builder badge, and PLUS**.

### Main Ways to Earn XP

| Action                       | Base XP                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Daily activity               | 20 XP                    |
| Partner check-in             | Up to 1,000 XP           |
| Buy or renew PLUS            | 400 XP                   |
| Referral buys or renews PLUS | 400 XP                   |
| Send OFE                     | 200 XP                   |
| Survey / preference question | 30–100 XP                |
| Crack the Word               | 10 XP + up to 100 XP     |
| Crack the Code               | 10 XP per eligible guess |
| Event RSVP                   | 50 XP                    |
| Cashback received            | 50 XP                    |
| Check-in feedback            | 15 XP                    |
| Shop discovery               | 10 XP                    |

Each action has its own rules and limits.

For example, partner check-ins usually have a waiting period, while sending OFE rewards XP only once every 30 days.

### Grow Your XP

There are four main ways to increase the XP generated by your activity.

#### 🔥 Flow

Come back every day and build your Flow.

Your Flow multiplier gradually increases until it reaches:

**×1.50 at 30 days**

Flow applies to selected daily activities and games.

→ **Learn more: Flow**

#### 💍 Rings

Create or join a Ring with other Ofero members.

An active Ring starts providing an XP bonus from 4 participants.

Regular members can reach:

**up to +45% XP**

A Ring manager can reach:

**up to +57% XP**

The Ring bonus applies to every XP action.

→ **Learn more: Rings**

#### 🛠 Builder

Build a chain of active PLUS members inside your network and unlock the Builder badge.

Builder gives:

**×1.25 XP**

The multiplier applies to every XP you earn, including eligible Network Bonus XP.

→ **Learn more: Builder**

#### ➕ PLUS

PLUS provides the largest direct XP multiplier:

**×2 XP**

It applies to almost every XP action and also improves your Ring and Network benefits.

→ **Learn more: PLUS**

### Multipliers Work Together

These bonuses are not isolated. They can build on top of each other.

The order is:

**Base XP → Flow → Ring → Builder → PLUS**

For example, a 20 XP daily action can become **110 XP** for a member with:

* ×1.50 Flow
* +45% Ring
* ×1.25 Builder
* ×2 PLUS

This is why two members can complete the same action and receive different amounts of XP.

### XP and OFE Rewards

XP determines your share of the daily OFE reward pool.

Your reward depends on your XP for that specific day compared with the XP earned by all eligible members that day.

**Daily Reward = Daily OFE Pool × (Your Daily XP ÷ Total Daily XP)**

Your lifetime XP is therefore a record of your activity, but your **daily XP** is what matters for each daily OFE distribution.

Stay active. Build your Flow. Use the ecosystem. Grow your network.

Your activity determines your share.
