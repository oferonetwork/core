# Price Lists

## F**eatures and Buttons**

1. **Create New List**:
   * Allows users to set up separate lists for specific purposes, such as "Groceries," "Electronics," or specific stores like "Kaufland" or "Carrefour."
   * Lists can be named based on user preferences.
2. **Rename List**:
   * Enables renaming existing lists for better organization.
3. **Add Product**:
   * Users can manually add product names and prices to their lists.
   * Includes real-time editing of both product names and prices directly within the list.
4. **Search Products**:
   * A simple search bar helps users quickly find specific products within a list.
5. **Prioritize Products**:
   * Users can prioritize specific items by marking them in the **Checked** column.
   * This feature is particularly useful for shopping, as it helps focus on the selected items from a list.
6. **Share List**:
   * Enables users to share their lists with others.
   * Shared lists are fully editable by the recipient.
7. **Copy List**:
   * Users can duplicate a friend's shared list into their own account for further use or modification.
8. **Delete List**:
   * Allows the removal of lists when they are no longer needed.

***
