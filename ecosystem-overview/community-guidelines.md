# Community Guidelines

{% hint style="info" %}
By participating in the Ofero Network, you agree to adhere to these principles and contribute to the growth of a supportive and respectful community.
{% endhint %}

***

### Core Principles

#### 1. **Respect and Inclusivity**

* Treat all members of the community with respect, regardless of background, experience level, or opinions.
* Avoid any form of discrimination, harassment, or hate speech.

#### 2. **Transparency and Honesty**

* Be honest and transparent in your interactions and contributions.
* Share accurate information and avoid spreading misinformation or engaging in deceitful practices.

#### 3. **Collaboration and Support**

* Foster a collaborative spirit by helping others and sharing knowledge.
* Support fellow members in navigating and benefiting from the Ofero Network.

#### 4. **Constructive Communication**

* Engage in discussions and debates respectfully.
* Provide constructive feedback and avoid personal attacks or inflammatory language.

***

### Code of Conduct

#### For General Members:

* Use the platform responsibly and ethically.
* Respect the privacy and confidentiality of other members.
* Refrain from spamming, self-promotion, or sharing inappropriate content.

#### For Leaders/Managers:

* Lead by example and uphold the values of the Ofero Network.
* Be accessible and supportive to those connected to your network.

***

### Guidelines for Governance Participation

#### 1. **Active Engagement**

* Participate in governance discussions and votes with a focus on the community’s best interests.

#### 2. **One SFT, One Vote**

* Adhere to the policy of one SFT per holder for governance and staking participation, ensuring fairness and equality.

#### 3. **Transparency in Decision-Making**

* Share your views openly and transparently to contribute to informed decision-making processes.

***

### Reporting and Resolving Issues

#### 1. **Reporting Violations**

* If you encounter behavior that violates these guidelines, report it by e-mail at [info@ofero.network](mailto:info@ofero.network) or via Telegram at [@oferonetwork](https://t.me/oferonetwork)&#x20;
* Provide detailed information to help the community moderators address the issue effectively.

#### 2. **Conflict Resolution**

* Attempt to resolve minor disputes respectfully and directly with the other party.
* For unresolved issues, seek assistance from community moderators.

***

### Consequences of Violating Guidelines

#### 1. **Warnings and Education**

* Minor violations may result in a warning or a reminder of the guidelines.
* Educational resources will be provided to help members understand expected behavior.

#### 2. **Temporary Restrictions**

* Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary restrictions on participation in community activities.

#### 3. **Permanent Removal**

* Persistent misconduct or egregious violations may result in permanent removal from the Ofero Network.

***

### Building a Stronger Community Together

These guidelines are a foundation for creating a thriving, respectful, and collaborative community. By upholding these principles, we can work together to ensure the success and growth of the Ofero Network. Thank you for being a valued member of our community!
