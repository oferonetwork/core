# The Ofero SFT

***

### Value Structure

* Each SFT is conceptually valued at **450,000 OFE**, aligned with the total OFE supply of 450 million tokens.
* The option to directly claim **100,000 OFE as an exit** is no longer available starting **February 28, 2025**.

### For Current Holders:

1. **Transition Option:** Holders preferring the original terms may request a full refund of **112,100 OFE per SFT** by **February 28, 2025**.
2. **Post-Deadline Transition:** After **February 28, 2025**, all SFTs automatically adopt the current valuation model. Refund requests will no longer be accepted.

***

### Reward Structure

#### Annual Percentage Rate (APR):

* The APR is set at **8.4%**, calculated on the valuation of **450,000 OFE per SFT**, ensuring sustainable long-term rewards.

#### Single Staking Mechanism:

* SFTs serve as the sole staking option within the Ofero Network.

***

### Ensuring Rewards and Decentralization

To promote sustainable rewards and decentralized governance, the following mechanisms are in place:

#### Accumulator Nexus Wallet:

* All network profits are directed exclusively to the **Accumulator Nexus Wallet** for distribution to SFT holders.

#### Aegis Wallet:

* The **Aegis Wallet** provides a safety net to ensure a minimum return for SFT holders:
  * Guarantees a **minimum APR of 6%** if no profits accumulate over a full year.
  * Supplements distributions to achieve the target APR of **8.4%** when profits are insufficient.

***

### Governance and Annual Meetups

#### Unique Holders Goal:

* By **July 1, 2026**, Ofero aims to have at least **800 unique SFT holders** actively participating in governance.

#### Registration for Rewards:

* Holders must register and verify their profiles on **ofero.me**.

#### One SFT per Holder for Governance and Staking:

* Staking and Governance participation is limited to **one SFT per holder** to maintain fairness.

#### Annual Meetups:

* Starting in **2026**, in-person governance meetups will be held at global locations. SFT holders can:
  * Vote on leadership roles.
  * Attend remotely through the governance dashboard on **ofero.me** if unable to join in person.

***

### VIP Status for SFT Holders

Owning an SFT grants **VIP status** within the Ofero Network, offering exclusive privileges and recognition.

***

### Decentralization Milestones

#### First Governance Meetup:

* Scheduled from **August 16, 2026**, to **August 23, 2026**.

#### Location Voting:

* The meetup location will be decided through a **community vote** on **March 7, 2026**.

***

### Benefits for SFT Holders

* **Sustainable Rewards:** Earn an APR based on the **450,000 OFE valuation**.
* **Active Participation:** Influence Ofero Network’s leadership through governance.
* **VIP Recognition:** Enjoy special privileges as part of the Ofero community.

***
