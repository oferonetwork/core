# General Questions

## **What is the long-term vision and strategy of the Ofero Network?**

The Ofero Network aims to create a blockchain-based financial ecosystem that seamlessly integrates real-world businesses with decentralized technology. Its long-term strategy includes expanding partnerships, growing its user base, and ensuring sustainable rewards through innovative mechanisms like its RAID 5-inspired blockchain structure and dual-chain operation on MultiversX and Binance Smart Chain.

## **What value does the Ofero Network bring to users and businesses?**

The Ofero Network connects users and businesses through:

* Loyalty rewards for users via the OFE token.
* Opportunities for businesses to list on platforms like Ofero.me and Ofero.ro, enhancing visibility and user engagement.
* Cashback solutions and blockchain-based interactions for real-world economic activities.

**What are the Tokenomics of the Ofero Network?**

The Ofero Network has a total supply of 450 million OFE tokens, operating on MultiversX and Binance Smart Chain. Revenue streams include token sales, listing fees for businesses on Ofero.me, and staking programs. The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for staking is designed for sustainability and long-term growth.

**4. How does Ofero Network ensure security?**

The Ofero Network uses a RAID 5-inspired blockchain architecture for enhanced security and redundancy. Additionally, it employs proprietary servers to ensure data integrity, and assets are protected through secure staking mechanisms and a robust governance framework.

**5. How can I add my business to Ofero.me?**

Businesses can be added automatically based on user engagement via the “Register Your Visit” feature. This allows users to notify businesses of their presence, and with sufficient interest, the Ofero team may reach out to negotiate partnerships.

**6. What is the “Register Your Visit” feature?**

This feature allows users to announce their presence at a location. It helps the Ofero Network identify potential partners and promotes businesses based on user interest.
