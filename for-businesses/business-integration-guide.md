### Introduction

Ofero.me is a cutting-edge on-chain financial ecosystem designed to foster loyalty, trust, and decentralized collaboration. Businesses can integrate with the Ofero Network to:

* Reward customers with Ofero tokens as loyalty points.
* Create exclusive discounts and special offers.
* Increase visibility within the growing Ofero community.

This guide will walk you through every step of the integration process.

***

### Benefits of Integration

By integrating your business with Ofero.me, you can:

* **Boost Customer Loyalty:** Reward customers with tokens that they can use within the ecosystem.
* **Increase Revenue:** Attract more customers through special offers and promotions.
* **Gain Insights:** Utilize blockchain transparency to understand customer behavior.
* **Strengthen Brand Trust:** Join a decentralized network built on trust and collaboration.

### Getting Started

#### Prerequisites

Before integrating with Ofero.me, ensure you have the following:

1. A registered business entity.
2. An active account on Ofero.me.
3. Basic understanding of blockchain and cryptocurrency (helpful but not mandatory).

#### Registration

If you haven’t already, register your business on Ofero.me by following these steps:

1. Visit [Ofero.me](https://ofero.me).
2. Click on **Sign Up** and select **Business Account**.
3. Complete the registration form and verify your email.

***

### Setting Up Your Business Profile

1. **Log in** to your Ofero.me account.
2. Navigate to the **Business Dashboard**.
3. Click **Create Profile** and fill in the required details, including:
   * Business name
   * Description
   * Contact information
   * Business logo and images
4. Save your profile to make it visible to users.

***

### Adding Loyalty Rewards

Loyalty rewards are central to the Ofero ecosystem. Follow these steps to set up your rewards system:

1. Go to the **Rewards** section in your dashboard.
2. Select **Add New Reward**.
3. Define the reward parameters:
   * Type of reward (e.g., token percentage per purchase).
   * Eligibility criteria (e.g., minimum spend).
4. Save and activate the reward.

Customers will automatically receive loyalty tokens when they meet the criteria.

***

### Creating Special Offers

Special Offers are instant discounts or promotions that enhance customer engagement. Here’s how to create one:

1. Navigate to the **Special Offers** section.
2. Click **New Offer**.
3. Fill in the details:
   * Offer name
   * Description
   * Discount percentage
   * Validity period
4. Publish the offer to make it live.

***

### User Interaction

Once integrated, customers can:

* Discover your business via the Ofero.me platform.
* Redeem loyalty rewards or take advantage of your special offers.
* Contact you for support or inquiries (your business profile will display your contact details).

#### Member Contact Information

To address potential issues efficiently, your profile will display both:

* **Business Contact Information:** For general inquiries and support.
* **Member Contact Information:** Linked to the individual who added the business to the platform, ensuring smooth communication.
